# Universal Templates

**English** · [Русский](README.md)

An open library of ready-to-use templates, checklists and AI prompts that are useful in any profession — from developers and designers to accountants, teachers and entrepreneurs.

Everything is written in Markdown. Copy a template, fill it in and use it right away in Notion, Obsidian, Google Docs, Word or a plain text file.

> The templates themselves are currently in Russian. English translations are welcome — see [Contributing](#contributing).

## What's inside

| Section | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| [Work](templates/work) | Everyday work tasks | Meeting notes, weekly report, project brief, retrospective |
| [Career](templates/career) | Job search and growth | Resume, cover letter, interview preparation |
| [Business](templates/business) | Running a business or freelancing | One-page business plan, commercial proposal, invoice |
| [Communication](templates/communication) | Emails and messages | Business emails: request, reminder, polite refusal, payment reminder |
| [Checklists](templates/checklists) | So nothing is forgotten | Employee onboarding, project launch, decision making, "5 Whys" |
| [Personal](templates/personal) | Life outside work | Monthly budget, yearly goals, travel checklist |
| [AI prompts](prompts) | Working with ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, etc. | Universal prompts and prompts for 12 professions |

## How to use

1. Find the template you need in the table above.
2. Open the file and click "Copy raw file" (or just select the text).
3. Paste it into your editor and replace the text in `[square brackets]` with your own.

## Principles

- **Universal.** Every template should work for people from different fields.
- **Concise.** Only what is actually used.
- **Practical.** Every template is tested in real work.
- **Free.** CC0 license: use it however you like, no attribution required.

## Contributing

Add a template, fix a mistake, translate a template into English or suggest an idea. The easiest way is to open an [issue](../../issues) using the "New template" form. Details are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) (in Russian).
